## Shared production corrections

Empty-result guidance now requires returned official references and explicitly forbids invented sources or URLs, after the full Qwen prose review found an unsupported purported legal-source recommendation. This result costs 44 native explanatory tokens across both copies; the overall risky-result maximum remains 60.

Default tool and field descriptions require a single initial discovery call and waiting for its result. The complete request preserves characters, punctuation, currentness and dates; JSON escapes must decode to the original characters, and callers must not append normalized dates or invented key/value filters. Discovery continuation requires an actual cursor in an earlier tool result in the conversation. Reading/index continuations use the same tool, copy the entire cursor including its signature, and omit original targets and dates. Full requested article/index reading continues without another permission question.

Risky result guidance puts unresolved existence before suffix-selection recovery, shortens HTTP messages while retaining status and unknown retryability, and tells an interrupted index to continue with its cursor. Coverage, failure position and the successful prefix remain in the structured result. Healthy complete direct reads gain no generic disclaimer. These are measured model-facing optimizations, not guarantees of arbitrary model behavior or external system-prompt changes.

## Review limitations

Call replay uses fresh default MCP registration with synthetic upstream controls, not live legal sources. Some unrelated exact discovery citations receive the fixture's TUEL identity and are correctly rejected as malformed; these replays establish contract behavior, not successful discovery of those laws. A generated call is not a completed legal answer or a multi-turn reading workflow. Every caller-visible prose output needs semantic inspection separately from automatic flags.

Denials of absence in the retained failed precursors sometimes include speculative HTTP causes, proposed broadening of exact searches, invented recovery links, or claims that a suffix was searched in text when the connector required an exact locator before retrieval. The passing targeted Qwen correction also contains inaccurate suffix-search provenance and speculative HTTP recovery advice. Current Qwen controls and the source correction additionally suggest checking a twelve-month update feed or reading a latest text to establish present legal status; neither alone proves it. Proposed citation repairs, broadened searches and recovery suggestions are not demonstrated connector capabilities or accepted legal-status proofs. These assertions and recovery paths are not verified connector facts or accepted source/provenance evidence. They remain visible in the traces; a denial of absence does not turn a mechanically failed precursor into a pass. Reference review covers both MCP-issued opaque locators/cursors and invented legal-source recommendations. A proposed URL absent tool evidence cannot be relabelled as accepted source evidence; the newest full Qwen sample remains a semantic failure on that basis. Finite passing samples do not guarantee downstream legal reasoning, verbatim reproduction, or arbitrary model behavior.

Inference requires the existing ignored OpenRouter environment file. Always decompress the newest cumulative report for `--prior-report`, plan first, and dispatch sequentially between reports under the unchanged **$1 OpenRouter cap** and overall **€10 ceiling**. `OPENROUTER_CASES` restricts a corrective sample, while omission retains the full 39-case/two-repetition matrix. `OPENROUTER_PAUSE_MS=5000` with `OPENROUTER_CONCURRENCY=1` adds a recorded five-second pause between provider requests after the observed 429s; zero remains the default. `OPENROUTER_MAX_TOKENS` may only select 1,520–1,536; the lower value exists solely to keep a complete matrix inside the cumulative cap, and any length-truncated output remains a failed row. This evaluation-only pacing and output bound add no retries and change no production Normattiva limits. Never reset the ledger or count provider failures as free. The fixture trust key is public test material, not a deployment credential.
